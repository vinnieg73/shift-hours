- **Timecard look (2026-10-16, replaces Liquid Glass).** The page reads like paper forms: a manila day card for the calculator, a ruled weekly timecard, and a travel voucher with a perforated trip stub for per diem. Labels are printed caps in IBM Plex Sans, every typed or computed number is Chivo Mono, chosen for its plain zero with no dot (Google Fonts, with preconnect and system fallbacks). Decimal hours are the main result, stamped in ink inside a straight double frame (Vinnie asked for no tilt), with h:mm underneath; both have Copy.
  - **One ink colour.** `--stamp` (the ink) and `--stamp-wash` (the opaque fill behind a selected box) are defined only in the marked INK block at the very top of the main stylesheet, with a light and a dark value. Nothing else hard-codes the ink, inline SVG included (it uses classes and `var()`). The block lists six options to swap in: Stamp Blue light #2B3A8C / #EAEAF0, dark #9DB2FF / #262A3A; Violet #5A2D8A / #EEE6F2 and #C7A6FF / #2E2638; Green #1F5B3A / #E4EDE4 and #8ED1A6 / #22302A; Teal #0B5A63 / #E2EDEC and #78CCD4 / #1F2F31; Rust #9A3F0C / #F4E7DA and #FF9F66 / #3A2A20; Carbon (in use, Vinnie's pick 2026-10-16) #1F1B16 / #E5E0D3 and #F3EDE0 / #36332D. With Carbon the light ink equals the body text colour, so selected boxes and this month in the lodging grid are told apart by their frame, wash fill and bold weight, not by text colour. Ink is used only for answers (decimal hours, week total, trip total), selected boxes, worked hours on the punch strip and this month in the lodging grid (framed and filled). Red (`--alert`) is only for errors and the "not counted" line; Clear and Clear week are grey.
  - **Light and dark follow the phone** (`prefers-color-scheme`): light is the manila day card, dark is a charcoal night-shift card. There is no in-page switch. Paper tokens (`--desk`, `--card`, `--paper`, `--card-deep`, `--rule`, `--rule-strong`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--ink-3`, `--alert`) sit right under the ink block; `theme-color` has a light and a dark meta.
  - **Flat by rule:** no backdrop-filter, blur, shadows or translucency anywhere, and no radius above 4px. Structure comes from 1px rules, box outlines, dotted leaders and double rules above totals.
  - **Punch strip instead of the dial.** The 24-hour SVG dial is now 24 punch cells (`#strip`): worked time is filled in ink with partial fills for the start and end hours, breaks are hatched, and Now is an ink caret plus a hairline (`#nowMark`, `#nowLine`). The scale reads 12a/6a/12p/6p/12a in 12-hour mode and 00/06/12/18/24 in 24-hour mode. `drawStrip()` builds it from the same result data the dial used; the legend still shows On the clock, Breaks and start -> end.
  - **Typed-value echo:** Start and End sit in a fill-in well with a small mono echo of how the entry was read (`845` prints `8:45`), `#startInEcho` / `#endInEcho`. It is hidden when it would repeat what was typed.
  - **Week as a ruled timecard:** DAY / IN / OUT / BRK / HRS columns, every input a boxed cell, an unfinished day's empty time outlined red (class `need`) with the note under it, the total under a double rule, then one red "not counted" line. On phones (≤640px) AM/PM is still one tap-to-flip chip per field, and the grid fits 402px with no wrapping.
  - **Per diem:** rates voucher with a grey double-ring FY stamp, lodging-by-month grid with the current month framed and filled in the ink wash, M&IE ledger lines with dotted leaders, and the trip total on a stub with a scalloped perforated edge. B/L/D chips taken off are struck through with a grey slash, never red.
  - Desktop (≥900px): entry and options on the left, the result on the right, the week full width below; per diem puts search and trip stub left, rates right.
- New visitors default to no break. There is no floating results bar on phones.
- **Decimal input:** a dot or comma in a time field means decimal hours. `9.5` is 9:30, `9.25` is 9:15, and `17.75` is 17:45 in 24-hour mode. A colon still means h:mm (`9:30`), and plain digits still work (`930`). Every decimal read shows a badge such as "Read 9.30 as 9:18", so a mistaken entry is visible.
- **Totals never include a guess.** In the calculator, a bad break (e.g. "7.5" minutes) or a start-and-end break with only one time pauses the total: it shows a dash, Copy is off, and a badge says why ("No total yet: add the end time of break 1"). Equal start and end show 0.00 with a note.
- **Weekly timesheet:** a card under the calculator with Mon to Sun rows (start, end, break minutes). Each day shows decimal hours with h:mm under it. The week total is the sum of minutes, shown as decimal hours with h:mm and a day count, and it has a Copy button. It uses the same clock format and rounding settings as the calculator. A day with something typed but missing a time says what is missing; a day with an invalid field shows field-named errors after the field loses focus. Either kind is left out of the total, the total dims, a red line names the days not counted, and week Copy stays off until they are finished or cleared. Zero-hour days are not counted as days. Clear week needs two taps. On phones (≤640px), each day takes two lines and AM/PM becomes one chip that flips when tapped.

- **Multiple tabs:** a tab writes only when its state differs from what it last read or wrote, and it takes other tabs' saves via the `storage` event (also re-read on becoming visible and on back/forward-cache restore). Unsaved edits in the current tab win. A save without a `week` key (from an old copy of the page) does not wipe the week rows.
- **24:00:** valid only in 24-hour mode. Switching to 12-hour leaves `2400` as typed and flags it; switching back restores it.
