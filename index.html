How it works
  • 12 PM is noon and 12 AM is midnight.
  • A dot or comma means decimal hours: 9.5 is 9:30 and 9.25 is 9:15. For clock minutes type 930 or 9:30.
  • If the end time is earlier than the start, the shift runs past midnight into the next day.
  • 15-minute rounding uses the 7-minute rule: 9:07 rounds down to 9:00, 9:08 rounds up to 9:15.
  • 5-minute rounding: :01–:02 round down, :03–:04 round up. 6-minute (tenth of an hour): :01–:02 down, :03–:05 up.
  • Decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60. For example 7:15 is 7.25 hours.
  • Typing tips:
  • The weekly timesheet uses the same clock format and rounding as above. Break is in minutes.
